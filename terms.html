5. Plans and payment

Free. The Free plan includes 20 orders each calendar month (Lagos time). After that, your store link keeps working, and new orders are sent to your WhatsApp as a message instead of being saved, until the 1st of the next month.

Pro. Pro costs ₦2,500 a month and removes the monthly order limit and the OrderPadi branding, and adds sales reports and order export. Payments are taken by Paystack. Pro renews automatically every month on the same date until you cancel. We never see or store your card details.

Cancelling. You can cancel any time in Settings, under Plan, with "Change card or cancel". You keep Pro until the end of the month you've paid for, then your account moves to Free. Your orders and customers stay in your account.

Price changes. If we change the price of Pro, we'll tell you at least 30 days before your next payment at the new price.
