Questions sellers ask

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. Your store link opens in any browser, on any phone. Customers pick what they want, add their name and address, and send the order.

Does OrderPadi collect payments for me?

No. Customers transfer straight to the bank account on your invoice, the way they already do. You confirm the money landed and mark the order paid.

What happens after my 20 free orders?

Your store link keeps working. Until the 1st of next month, a customer's order comes to your WhatsApp as a ready-made message instead of being saved. Upgrade to Pro any time for unlimited orders.

How do I add product photos?

Open a product and tap Add photo. Pick one from your gallery or take one with your camera. OrderPadi shrinks it on your phone first, so it uploads quickly and loads fast for customers.

Can I add orders that came in on WhatsApp or Instagram DM?

Yes. Add an order by hand in a few taps, including items that aren't on your store, a discount or a delivery fee, then send the invoice like any other.

Can new customers find my store?

Yes. Turn on your store profile and you're listed in the OrderPadi store directory, where shoppers search by what they want and where they are. Ratings from your customers show there too.

Will it work on my phone?

It's made for phones first. Open OrderPadi in your browser and add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app.

How do I cancel Pro?

Go to Settings, then Plan, and tap "Change card or cancel". You keep Pro until the end of the month you've paid for, then move back to Free. Your orders stay where they are.
