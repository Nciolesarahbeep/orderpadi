Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information OrderPadi collects, why we need it, who we share it with and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. "We" and "us" mean OrderPadi. If anything here isn't clear, contact us using the details at the end.

1. Information we collect

From sellers who create an account:

  • your business name, store link and email address;
  • your password, which we store only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody at OrderPadi can read it;
  • your WhatsApp number and the bank name, account name and account number you choose to show on invoices;
  • your products, prices, delivery fee and notes;
  • the orders, payments and customers you record.

From customers who order from a store: name, phone number, delivery address, what they ordered and any note they add. Sellers can also add these details when they record an order by hand.

For Pro payments: Paystack handles the payment. We receive confirmation that you paid, the amount, the date, and the reference codes Paystack gives your subscription. We never see or store card numbers.

To keep the service safe: the internet (IP) address a request comes from, used to stop abuse such as repeated wrong passwords, mass sign-ups or fake orders.

2. Cookies and storage on your device

We use one cookie, which keeps you logged in to your seller account. On a store page, the customer's phone remembers their basket and their name, phone number and address, so they don't have to type them again next time. That stays on their own device. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use information

  • to run your store, send orders to you, and show invoices and receipts to your customers;
  • to send sign-up, login and password reset codes by email;
  • to take and manage Pro payments;
  • to prevent fraud, abuse and attacks on the service;
  • to answer your questions and fix problems;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We use information because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping it secure, or because the law requires it.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use a small number of companies to run OrderPadi, and they only process information on our instructions:

  • Netlify, which hosts the website and app;
  • Neon, which hosts our database;
  • Resend, which sends our emails;
  • Paystack, which processes Pro payments.

Some of these companies store information on servers outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires. We will also share information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

5. Sellers and their customers

When a customer orders from a store, the seller decides how to use that customer's details, and OrderPadi stores and processes them on the seller's behalf. Sellers agree to use customer details only to serve their orders. If you are a customer and want your details changed or removed, contact the seller, or contact us and we'll help.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, store and order information: for as long as the account is open. When an account is closed, we delete it within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.
  • Login and sign-up codes: they expire after 10 minutes.
  • Security records of login attempts: about one day.

7. How we protect it

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, login sessions can be ended from any device, repeated wrong passwords are blocked, and access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong we'll act quickly and tell those affected as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct information that's wrong (sellers can change most of it in Settings);
  • delete your information or close your account;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose, or move it elsewhere.

We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

OrderPadi is for sellers aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we'll tell sellers by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
